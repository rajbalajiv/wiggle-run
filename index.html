      A SMALL CHALLENGE FOR ROBOT OWNERS

      how to play.

      One robot. One colored marker. Four progressively harder courses. Get as far as you can without leaving the lines.

      1. Point a steady camera at your robot's work area.
      2. Attach a bright colored marker to the gripper. Connect the camera and click the marker once.
      3. Choose a course, enter a name, then select Enable run.
      4. Move through START to begin automatically. Follow the red corridor.
      5. Crossing the boundary ends your run. Post your score to the global board.
      You control the robot locally. Keep movements within its safe range. Hold your camera steady.
